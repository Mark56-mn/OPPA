Low-end Android first

Trust & Safety

Device-bound sessions

Sessions only work on registered devices. Lost a phone? Revoke it from any other signed-in device.

Step-up for money

Transfers and payment reversals require a fresh device-verified confirmation bound to the exact amount and recipient.

Report and block

Report abusive accounts confidentially and block anyone instantly. Reports go to a human review queue.

Least-privilege operations

Staff access is role-gated and audited. Operators cannot casually read your private conversations.

Privacy

What we collect

Your phone number (to verify your account), your profile details, and the minimum technical data needed to run the service securely.

What we never do

We never sell your data, never ask for your OTP, and never place your tokens or secrets in notification content.

Your controls

Manage notification preferences, sign out devices, and request account deletion through support.
